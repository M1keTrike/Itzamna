# Brag Plan: Proyecto 8D

## 1. Project Information
- **Name**: Proyecto 8D
- **Core Value**: Algorithmic resource distribution and governance for extreme scarcity (Mars).
- **Target Audience**: Judges, tech audience, general public.

## 2. Creative Angle
- **Tone**: Cinematic, dramatic, and inspiring.
- **Hook**: The contrast between a critical resource alert and a smooth, automated resolution.
